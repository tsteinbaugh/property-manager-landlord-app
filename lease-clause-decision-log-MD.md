## 2. Tag-first results (rules 26-28)
### 2.1 Tagged MD as written (47)
Each tag note starts "MD: Tagged as written (tag-first screen, rules 26-28, MD log §2.1)" and gives the full reason. Fee clauses were screened against § 8-208(d)(11) (nothing beyond the deposit and first month's rent to start the lease), the § 8-203(a)(3) definition of a security deposit, and the abuse-victim lock-change rule (§ 8-5A-05). Liability language was screened against § 8-105. The reasons below are the MD note segments, without the closing "Rule 15" sentence.
- `early-termination-ks` (clause): No Maryland statute regulates a negotiated early-termination option or fee; the clause preserves every statutory exit by its own terms, which in Maryland are the servicemember change-of-assignment cap (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-212.1(b)), the medical certification cap of 2 months' rent (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-212.2(c)) and the abuse-victim termination (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-5A-02). Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-212.2(b) displaces the medical cap only where the lease's early-termination clause 'Requires written notice to vacate of 1 month or less' and imposes liability of 2 months' rent or less; the fee here can exceed that, so the statutory cap stays available (`edu-medical-termination-md`). The mitigation duty in Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-207 cannot be waived; the clause does not purport to. Case law on the fee as a penalty not searched. rule_type REQUIRED is the shared value; in Maryland the clause is optional (RECOMMENDED).
- `possession-delay-ca` (clause): Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-204(c): if the landlord fails to deliver possession at the beginning of the term 'the rent payable under the lease shall abate until possession is delivered', and the tenant 'on written notice to the landlord before possession is delivered, may terminate, cancel, and rescind the lease'; (d) the landlord is then liable for 'all money or property given as prepaid rent, deposit, or security'. This variant matches; shared `possession-delay` is not tagged because it delays the tenant's termination right for 30 days. The consequential-damages right in § 8-204(e) is untouched (`edu-possession-delay-md`).
- `rental-application-accuracy` (clause): Maryland has no statute on false rental applications (`edu-no-rental-application-accuracy-md`); a material misrepresentation is a contract breach the clause records. From October 1, 2026 a landlord with five or more Maryland units may not ask about most criminal history before a conditional offer (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-2A-05(a)); the clause's last sentence excludes information the landlord 'was not permitted by law to request or consider'.
- `surrender-end-of-term` (clause): No Maryland statute sets a surrender procedure; the landlord's own notice before the end of a stated term (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-402(c)(2), 60 days for a written lease of more than 1 week or a month-to-month tenancy) is kept by 'unless applicable law entitles Tenant to remain' (`edu-end-of-term-notice-md`). Treating property left behind as abandoned is limited to a lease that 'has been terminated' and property 'abandoned by the tenant' (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(6)); the clause applies only after Tenant vacates at the end of the Lease and 'to the extent permitted by applicable law' (`edu-abandoned-property-md`).
- `hoa-compliance` (clause): Fee screen (kickoff): Maryland has no closed list of allowed tenant fees; the only move-in amount limit is Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(11) (deposit plus first month's rent 'in order to commence the lease and occupy'), which a later association fine does not touch. Montgomery County leases of association property must state that the owner's association obligations bind the tenant (`montgomery-association-docs-md`).
- `lead-based-paint` (clause): Federal disclosure (40 C.F.R. 745.113); Maryland adds its own tenant-rights notice, inspection certificate and information packet for pre-1978 rentals (`lead-risk-reduction-md`), turnover duties (`edu-lead-risk-reduction-turnover-md`), and has no separate state lead warning statement (`edu-lead-tenant-remedies-md`).
- `appliances-included` (clause): Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(c)(2) requires the lease to state the parties' 'specific obligations as to heat, gas, electricity, water, and repair' (`utility-repair-obligations-md`); listing appliances is compatible. Listing air-conditioning matters: in apartment buildings of 10 or more units, a unit whose 'lease required the landlord to provide air-conditioning' at any time on or after January 1, 2025 must have air-conditioning that keeps (or, if tenant-controlled, can keep) each habitable space at 80 degrees or below from June 1 to September 30, except on National Register property (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-122(b)(2)(ii), (c); `edu-air-conditioning-duty-md`).
- `landlord-maintenance` (clause): Consistent with the warranty of habitability (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-212(c)) and rent escrow for serious defects (§ 8-211). The clause's improper-use exception tracks the statutory defense where 'the tenant, the tenant's family, agent, employees, assignees, or social guests caused' the condition (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-212(h)(1)). Maryland's tenant notice may be certified mail, actual notice or an agency violation notice (§ 8-211(f)(2)); the clause's request for written notice does not displace that (`edu-repair-notice-md`).
- `services-utilities-provided-ks-oh` (clause): Used instead of shared `services-utilities-provided`, whose sentence that Landlord 'is not liable for any interruption' would fall within Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-105 (a lease provision that would 'preclude or exonerate the landlord from any liability' for the landlord's fault is 'void').
- `utilities-paid-by-landlord` (clause): Satisfies part of Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(c)(2) (`utility-repair-obligations-md`). Where gas or electric service to a unit with its own single meter (not a master meter) is in the landlord's name and the lease requires the landlord to pay it, the tenant may open an account in its own name if service is threatened with or subject to termination, and may deduct payments made to the utility, or a required utility deposit, from rent (Md. Code Ann., Pub. Util. § 7-309(b)(2), (c), (g); Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-212.3(b); `edu-utilities-paid-by-landlord-md`).
- `addendum-precedence` (clause): Maryland's required attachments (the Maryland Tenants' Bill of Rights, Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(c)(4)) are disclosures 'that applicable law requires' and control under the clause's own exception. No Maryland statute requires an optional addendum to control over the lease (`edu-no-addendum-precedence-md`).
- `entire-agreement` (clause): No Maryland statute lets a landlord amend a residential lease by notice except the rent-increase notice (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-209(b)); the clause's 'as applicable law permits' limb reaches only that (`edu-no-entire-agreement-md`).
- `governing-law` (clause): No Maryland choice-of-law rule for leases was found (`edu-no-governing-law-md`).
- `notices` (clause): Maryland prescribes delivery methods for several notices (rent increase, Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-209(b)(2); entry, § 8-221(c)(3); pre-filing nonpayment notice, § 8-401(c)(2); warrant notice, § 8-407(b)(1)); the clause yields to each ('that requirement will control'). A lease may not require electronic delivery of rent-increase notices (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(9)); the clause designates no alternative method (`edu-notice-delivery-methods-md`).
- `severability` (clause): Lawful as written; it cannot save a prohibited term because tendering a lease containing one exposes the landlord to 'actual damages incurred as a reason thereof, including reasonable attorney's fees' (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(g)(2); `edu-no-severability-md`).

No Maryland statute bars requiring renter's insurance (`edu-no-renters-insurance-rules-md`); if a landlord collects a premium at move-in it counts toward the move-in limit (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(11)).
- `assigned-parking-space` (clause): No Maryland statute on assigned residential parking (`edu-no-assigned-parking-space-md`).
- `parking-ks-oh-ca` (clause): Used instead of shared `parking`, whose 'not liable for damage to or theft of a vehicle' sentence would be void under Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-105 to the extent of the landlord's own fault.

- `assistance-animal-accommodation` (clause): Consistent with Maryland fair housing law, which bars disability discrimination in rental terms (Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 8-401(c)) is not a demand the clause excludes ('without demand' refers to the monthly payment). Deductions the law permits (utility payments under Md. Code Ann., Real Prop. § 14-128; `edu-us-flag-display-md`); the clause's last sentence preserves it. The consent requirement may not be used to prohibit clotheslines on single-family property (detached homes, townhouses, and condominium, HOA or cooperative property, not property with more than four units; historic-register property excepted) (Md. - `smoking-policy` (clause): Maryland's indoor-air law does not reach private residences and no statute bars a lease ban on smoking or vaping, including cannabis (`edu-no-smoking-policy-md`, `edu-cannabis-home-grow-md`).

- **Identify browser downloads by SHA-256, not name.** Blob downloads from the built-in browser land as `.tmp` files in Downloads; staging them and matching by size and hash worked every time, and a small final "flush" download made the last real file appear.
