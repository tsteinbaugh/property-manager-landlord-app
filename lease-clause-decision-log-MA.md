### 6.3 Drafting and legal decisions made by Claude (recorded, not asked)
- **Smoking and marijuana vaping:** ch. 94G, § 2(d)(1) bars a lease from prohibiting non-smoked marijuana consumption; ch. 94G doesn't define smoking, and ch. 270, § 22's vaping definition is tobacco-only. `smoking-policy-ma` bans smoking anything and vaping tobacco or nicotine, and leaves marijuana vaporizing to the statute.
- **No returned-check fee:** Massachusetts sets no lease fee; its remedy is the ch. 93, § 40A demand; a fee charged because rent bounced within 30 days of the due date risks the § 15B(1)(c) penalty bar.
- **Broker fees:** `due-at-signing-ma` bars payments to the lessor or its agent beyond the closed list and does not bar a fee the tenant pays to a broker the tenant alone engaged (ch. 112, § 87DDD½).
- **Deposit return clock:** `security-deposit-return-ma` states both starting points of § 15B(4) (end of occupancy for a tenancy at will, end of the stated term for a fixed-term lease), after round 1 found a single "tenancy ends" ambiguous.
- **Existing condition:** the shared "good order and repair" acknowledgment is replaced by the statutory statement of condition, because a conflicting term is void and enforcing it forfeits the deposit (§ 15B(6)(c), (8)).
- **Utilities:** the tenant pays only separately metered electricity or gas, a dedicated oil tank and services, with water only under § 22 and common-area lights only under 410.300(F); everything else stays with the owner (410.200, 410.210, ch. 186, § 14).
- **Fire relocation notice:** the lease can serve as the written notice "at the beginning of the lease or tenancy period" (ch. 175, § 99, cl. Fifteenth A); the use note covers multi-unit buildings, and non-signing occupants should get a copy.
- **Snow and ice:** 410.260(D)(4) lets a written agreement that clearly identifies them make the occupant responsible for exits under the occupant's exclusive control, but (D)(2) keeps exterior stairways, fire escapes and egress balconies with the owner; `snow-removal-ma` lists the areas by hand and keeps those structures with the owner in any multi-unit building.
- **House rules:** `rules-ma` binds the tenant to new rules only with written acceptance or at renewal, because a mid-term substantial alteration within six months of protected activity is presumed retaliatory (§ 18).
- **Rent concession:** no clawback on default (940 CMR 3.17(6)(c)), and losing a concession for late rent could be a § 15B(1)(c) penalty.
- **Collection costs:** allowed only for amounts more than 30 days past due, capped at a landlord figure, with attorney fees left to the default clause and § 20.
- **Assistance animals:** the shared clause is tagged although Massachusetts law states no direct-threat exception in its text, because the clause applies the exception narrowly; the open question is recorded (§2.1, §10).
- **Entry notice:** `landlords-access` is tagged; its "longer notice if the law requires" picks up the 48-hour code-work notice in 105 CMR 410.003(E).
- **Expunged records:** `rental-application-accuracy-ma` protects a "no record" answer or silence about an expunged record as a contract term (ch. 276, § 100N covers employment only; battery #390 found no housing counterpart).

## Proposed SOP changes

- **Save canvass output after every topic, and run at most two canvass agents at once.** Six parallel canvass agents hit the session usage limit before any wrote output, and their work was lost; the rerun (two at a time, one JSON line per topic, skip topics already saved) finished all 341 topics.
- **Find sections a site files outside their chapter index by searching the whole corpus by heading, not only by chapter.** malegislature.gov lists ch. 239, § 17 only in chapter 221's index; a chapter-scoped crawl or citation lookup misses it.
- **Address malegislature.gov fractional sections with a space before the fraction** (`/api/Chapters/112/Sections/87DDD%201~2`), as the site's own links do; the unspaced form returns 400.
- **Crawl mass.gov single-threaded with a pause and a long back-off on 403.** Three parallel workers triggered a site-wide 403 for about two minutes; one worker with a 0.7 s gap and a 90 s back-off finished 1,488 regulation pages with no failure.
- **Have a fix-proposing checker quote every subsection its proposed wording relies on.** In Massachusetts, round 2 proposed wording on the two-family exemption that misread § 4(11), and round 3 caught it only by reading the subsection; when a fix changes a fair-housing exemption, re-check the sibling rows on the same topic.
- **Run a script that flags any row citing a battery whose positives failed, unless the note says so.** Two Massachusetts rows cited failed first runs; the check takes seconds over `batteries.jsonl`.
- **Write single-quoted text in notes only for statute or regulation wording; put quotations of the library's own clauses in double quotes.** The quote checker can't find clause wording in a statute and reports it as a failure.
