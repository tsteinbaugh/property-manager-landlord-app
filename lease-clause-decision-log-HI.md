### §6.3 Legal and drafting judgments (rule 76; made, not asked)
- **Yard entry** is treated as entry to the dwelling unit for the two-day notice and closed purpose list (HRS § 521-53); `landlords-access-hi` says so (canvass C2 ruling).
- **EV charging:** shared EV clauses are not tagged; HRS § 196-7.5 protects owners only and gives a tenant no installation right (canvass C2 ruling).
- **Association fees or deposits at the start of a tenancy** are not offered as tenant charges: the landlord 'may not require or receive from or on behalf of a tenant at the beginning of a rental agreement any money other than the money for the first month's rent and a security deposit' (HRS § 521-44(b)) (canvass C2 ruling).
- **`rent-concession-wi`** tagged HI (a neutral record of each concession, no clawback; the 8% cap of HRS § 521-21(f) is explained in `edu-no-rent-concession-rule-hi`).
- **Arbitration:** no Hawaii arbitration clause is offered. A lease term that sends code remedies to arbitration risks being read as an agreement to 'waive or agree to forego rights or remedies under this chapter' (HRS § 521-31(a)), and case notes report lease arbitration terms held unconscionable (cases unread). Education only (`edu-lease-arbitration-hi`); rule 6 (one curated answer).
- **Automatic renewal:** whether HRS § 481-9.5 reaches a residential lease is unsettled; `automatic-renewal-hi` builds in every § 481-9.5 requirement, the cautious course.
- **Longer tenant notice:** checked every HI clause and tagged clause for a tenant notice period; none requires a month-to-month tenant to give more than the 28 days of HRS § 521-71(b). The 45-day notice in `security-deposit-use-hi` is the statute's own condition for applying the deposit to the last month's rent (HRS § 521-44(b)); the 30-day notice in `early-termination-ks` is an option to end a fixed Term early.
- **Adverse-proceeding reporting clause:** not offered; no Hawaii statute backs one (`edu-no-adverse-proceeding-notice-hi`).
- **Lease-copy penalty:** whether the § 521-67 penalty reaches a missing lease copy (HRS § 521-43(d)) is unsettled; rows say "may".
- **Owner move-in end of a fixed term:** offered as optional (`owner-occupancy-termination-hi`, CONDITIONAL, SERVES_LANDLORD; at least 45 days' notice; not usable while an emergency proclamation limits terminations under HRS § 127A-30(a)(2)). Hawaii has no just-cause rule, so this is a contract right for the landlord (rule 54).
- **Periodic notice periods** (`periodic-tenancy-notice-hi`) are a CONSTRAINED_TERM clause, not education: the lease states the landlord's notice as a figure of at least 45 days and the fixed statutory tenant, conversion and 10-day periods (HRS § 521-71).
- **Lockout "with cause":** HRS § 521-63(c) penalises removal 'without cause or without court order' and doesn't define a removal with cause; rows treat every lockout as unlawful.
- **Nonpayment mediation wait:** HRS § 521-68(c) ties the 20-day filing wait to mediation being scheduled and the tenant taking part, while the bold warning in § 521-68(b)(7) and the Judiciary's form tie it only to scheduling; rows tell landlords to wait the full 20 days whenever mediation is scheduled (rule 39; §7 legal watch).
- **Entity landlords in court:** `edu-entity-landlord-representation-hi` raised to VERIFIED after the main pass read Rules of the District Courts Rule 4 and searched the Rules of the Supreme Court in the browser (§1.3); its battery was rerun (HI-M-ENTITY-01) because C4's run failed a real positive.
- **Employee housing clause** (`employee-housing-occupancy-hi`) kept as optional: it records in writing that occupancy is conditional on employment, the fact on which HRS § 521-7(6) excludes the tenancy from the code, and its opener warns against use 'solely to avoid the application of this chapter' (HRS § 521-7). The rest of a builder lease then binds as agreed contract terms, which only adds protections for the employee.
- **Lease with an option to buy:** one row (`edu-lease-option-disclosure-hi`, topic required-disclosures) on the ch. 508D seller disclosure that a 'lease with option to buy' triggers (HRS § 508D-1); no lease-option clause is offered (sales are outside the library).

- **Advertising ban and owner-occupant exemptions:** the § 515-4 exemptions say only that § 515-3 does not apply; rows treat the advertising, form and inquiry ban (§ 515-16(7)) and § 515-6 as still reaching exempt owners (unsettled; cautious course). The § 368F-3 source-of-income exemptions do reach its advertising ban.
- **Detached ADUs:** whether the owner-occupied two-family exemptions (§§ 515-4(a)(1), 368F-3(4)) reach a house plus a detached ADU is unsettled; rows advise treating the rental as covered.
- **Severability:** the tagged `severability` row stands: ch. 521 voids offending terms one by one and a plain-language failure does not void the lease (HRS § 487A-2). HRS § 480-12 voids a contract 'in violation of this chapter' (ch. 480); no residential case was searched (§7).
