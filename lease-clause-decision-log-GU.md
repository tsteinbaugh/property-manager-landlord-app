### §2.2 Clauses read in the main pass and not tagged, with reasons
**Class decisions (rule 52 and the Step D screens; rows cite them as "log §2.2 class decision N"):**
1. **Exculpation.** 'All contract which have for their object, directly or indirectly, to exempt anyone from responsibility for his own fraud, or willful injury to the person or property of another, or violation of law, whether willful or negligent, are against the policy of the law.' (18 GCA § 88102), and the Act's rights, including the landlord's code and repair duties (21 GCA § 48202), can't be waived (21 GCA § 48105). Shared clauses with an unqualified "Landlord is not liable" disclaimer are not tagged; Guam uses the no-disclaimer variants (`tenants-property-insurance-ks-oh-ca`, `parking-ks-oh-ca`, `storage-space-ks-oh-ca`, `services-utilities-provided-ks-oh`).
2. **Liquidated damages.** A sum fixed in advance for a breach 'is to that extent void' (18 GCA § 88103) unless 'from the nature of the case, it would be impracticable or extremely difficult to fix the actual damage' (18 GCA § 88104). Fixed late, early-termination, holdover, lease-break and re-letting fees are not tagged as written; Guam clauses that offer one recite the § 88104 basis, and their education rows say a court decides on the facts and case law this pass didn't search. A charge for a service actually performed at its actual cost is not liquidated damages.
3. **Move-in money.** 'Landlords may not collect more than the first month's rent and security deposit (including pet deposit if appropriate) at the time of lease commencement.' (21 GCA § 48201(a)). Clauses collecting anything else at commencement (last month's rent, second deposits, key or remote deposits, non-refundable or administrative fees, pet fees other than the pet deposit) are not tagged. Pet rent is rent (21 GCA § 48102(n)). Application fees taken before commencement are outside the sentence's words; `edu-application-fees-gu` says so.
4. **Deposit cap and pet deposit.** Deposit ≤ one month's rent; a separate 'reasonable "pet deposit,"' up to $500.00 per pet (21 GCA § 48201(a)).
5. **Working days.** 'days are working days, and do not include weekends and federal and local Guam government holidays' (21 GCA § 48102(f)) for every Chapter 48 period; periods outside Chapter 48 are calendar days computed under 1 GCA § 709. Every row that counts days says which kind (kickoff).
6. **Notice methods and e-notice.** Notice to a tenant is 'personally tendered to the tenant or mailed by registered or certified mail to the place of the tenant's known residence' (21 GCA § 48103(b)(2)); unlawful-detainer notices follow 21 GCA § 21105; where another law specifies a method, an electronic record 'must be sent, communicated, or transmitted by the method specified in the other law' (18 GCA § 91108(b)). E-mail can be a courtesy copy, never the required notice; `notices` and `electronic-notice-ma` are tagged because they work that way.
7. **Nonpayment and breach notices: two regimes coexist.** The Act's nonpayment notice (pay within five working days; the landlord 'may terminate the rental agreement thirty (30) days after receipt of notice', 21 GCA § 48401(b)) and noncompliance notice (§ 48401(a)) sit beside the eviction chapter's five-day pay-or-quit and three-day perform-or-quit notices (21 GCA § 21103(b)-(c)). § 48101 makes the Act prevail only over 18 GCA §§ 51101-51112. No Guam case reconciling them was searched. The rows' course: one written notice that satisfies both, served by a method valid under both (personal delivery), with the Act's working-day periods. Shared default clauses with a shorter cure or notice are not tagged.
8. **Attorney's fees are mutual by statute.** 'Any provision for costs or attorneys' fees in any agreement, contract, or lease shall, in the interest of fairness, be construed as being mutual …' (5 GCA § 32203; GU-M-AF02 after AF01's positive failed). The brief's first version said fees run only where the Act grants them; TA's lead corrected it before any row relied on it. Fee clauses are written as mutual on their face (`attorney-fees-gu`, `collection-costs-gu`).
9. **No repair self-help.** A tenant may not repair 'without the express written consent of landlord' or deduct 'unless approved in writing', and 'Tenant may not withhold rent for repairs not done.' (21 GCA § 48303(a)-(b)); the exceptions are essential services (§ 48304) and typhoon weatherheads (§ 48307). 18 GCA § 51102's repair-and-deduct yields (§ 48101).
10. **Snow and cold weather:** Guam has none; those topics are Not applicable.
11. **Articles 5 and 6 can't be waived** (P.L. 35-093:3; P.L. 38-092:3); clauses touching early termination, locks, guests, screening or payment sources keep them by name.
